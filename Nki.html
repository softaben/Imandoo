Rules Strategy London Session Risk Management

Trading Strategy Overview

Primary Strategy: ICT (Inner Circle Trader) Scalping during London Session

Trading Pairs: EURUSD & GBPUSD only

Session Focus: 07:00-16:00 GMT (London Session)

Primary Kill Zones: 07:00-08:00 GMT & 10:00-11:00 GMT

Entry Conditions

Trade only during London Session hours
Wait for H1 premium/discount alignment
ICT model confirmation (FVG, OB, LS)
M5/M1 entry confirmation
Minimum 1:2 risk/reward ratio

Risk Management Rules

Maximum 1% risk per trade
Maximum 3% daily loss limit
Maximum 3 trades per session
Stop loss always set before entry
No trading 5 min before/after high impact news

ICT Model Rules

FVG (Fair Value Gap) Rules

  • Wait for 3-candle formation on M5 or M15
  • Enter on retest of FVG boundaries
  • Stop loss below/above FVG
  • Take profit at next liquidity zone

Order Block Rules

  • Identify on H1 or M15 timeframe
  • Enter on break and retest of OB
  • Stop loss below/above OB
  • Target previous swing high/low

Liquidity Sweep Rules

  • Identify liquidity pools on HTF
  • Enter on reversal confirmation
  • Stop loss beyond liquidity sweep
  • Target opposite liquidity zone

Prohibited Actions

No trading outside London Session hours
No trading during high impact news (red folder)
No revenge trading after losses
No position size increases during losing streak
No trading without clear ICT setup

Common Psychological Pitfalls

FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)

Symptoms: Entering trades late, chasing price, overtrading

Solution: Wait for proper setup, stick to trading plan, maximum 3 trades per session

Revenge Trading

Symptoms: Increasing position size after losses, trading without setups

Solution: Take a break after 2 consecutive losses, stick to 1% risk rule

Impatience

Symptoms: Early entries, cutting winners short, moving stop losses

Solution: Use checklist before every trade, set alerts for entries

Psychology Improvement Plan

Meditate for 10 minutes before trading session
Review trading plan before first trade
Rate emotional state before/after each trade
Take 15-minute break after 2 consecutive losses
